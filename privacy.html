Privacy Policy

Your chats are personal. We treat them that way.

This Privacy Policy explains what the Lunova: AI Girlfriend & Chat app ("Lunova", package com.lunova.app) collects, why, who receives it, how long we keep it and how you can have it deleted.

App: Lunova: AI Girlfriend & Chat Developer: Xypher Labs Effective: 9 October 2026

No account needed

We never ask for your email, phone number, password or contacts. The app uses a random ID created on your phone.

Chats power the replies

Your messages are stored on our server and sent to our AI provider so your companion can answer and remember the conversation.

We do not sell your data

Chat content is never sold and never used to target ads. The free plan shows ads from Google AdMob; paid plans show none.

You can delete it

Clear everything on your phone in one tap, and ask us to erase the server copy at any time. See how.

1Who we are

Lunova is published by Xypher Labs ("we", "us"). We decide how and why the information described here is handled, which makes us the data controller (in India, the data fiduciary) for the app.

Lunova lets you chat, exchange voice notes and make voice calls with fictional companions that are generated by artificial intelligence. They are not real people.

Questions about privacy can be sent to xypherlabs.dev@gmail.com.

3What we do not collect

  • Your email address, phone number or password (unless you email us yourself)
  • Your contacts, call log or SMS
  • Your precise location
  • Photos, videos or files other than the ones you pick and send in a chat
  • Recordings of your voice calls
  • Payment card details

Lunova does not listen through the microphone in the background. The microphone is used only while you are recording a voice note or are on a voice call.

4How we use your information

  • To run the app: produce companion replies, voice calls and AI photos, keep your chat history and suggest companions.
  • To send notifications: let you know when a companion has written to you. You can turn these off in your phone's settings at any time.
  • To manage plans: apply the limits of the free plan and of paid plans, and confirm purchases.
  • To keep the service safe: prevent abuse, enforce our Terms, review reports and block prohibited content.
  • To improve Lunova: measure which features are used and fix crashes.
  • To show ads on the free plan.
  • To meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on: performing our agreement with you (running the app), your consent (microphone, notifications, personalised ads, and any sensitive details you choose to write in a chat), our legitimate interests (safety, analytics, improving the app) and legal obligations.

5How the AI handles your chats

Every companion in Lunova is an AI character. Replies, voices and photos are generated by software. No human is writing to you.

  • To produce a reply, our server sends your message, recent conversation history, your nickname and the companion's character description to our AI provider. Photos, voice notes and files you send are passed on in the same way.
  • Voice calls are streamed in real time between your phone, our server and the AI provider.
  • AI photos of a companion are generated from a text description of the scene. Your own photos are not used to create them.
  • We do not use your chats to train our own AI models.
  • Automated systems process your chats. People at Xypher Labs may look at stored conversations only when needed to investigate a report, a safety or abuse issue, a technical fault or a legal request.

Please keep sensitive details out of your chats. Do not share passwords, bank or card numbers, government ID numbers, or health details you would not want processed by an AI service. AI replies can be wrong and are not professional advice.

7Ads and analytics

  • The free plan shows banner and full-screen ads served by Google AdMob. Paid plans show no ads.
  • AdMob uses your device's advertising ID and similar information to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, including in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your choice through Google's consent screen before any ad is requested.
  • The content of your chats is never shared with advertisers and never used to choose ads.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android under Settings → Google → Ads (the wording varies by device), and manage personalised ads at adssettings.google.com.
  • Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

9How long we keep it

  • Chats, preferences, plan and usage data on our server: kept while you use Lunova, until you ask us to delete them.
  • Server backups: made daily and deleted after 14 days.
  • Photos, voice notes and files you send, and call audio: not stored on our server.
  • Data on your phone: stays until you reset your profile in the app, clear the app's storage or uninstall it.
  • Analytics and crash data: kept by Firebase for the periods set in its services, up to 14 months for analytics and 90 days for crash reports.
  • Records we must keep by law (for example purchase records) are kept for as long as the law requires.

10Delete your data

You can remove your data without giving a reason and without paying anything.

On your phone

  1. Open Lunova and go to Profile.
  2. Choose Reset profile. This clears your name, preferences, favourites and chats from the phone.
  3. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, also removes everything stored on the phone.

On our servers

  1. Email xypherlabs.dev@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Lunova data, from any email address.
  2. Because Lunova has no accounts, we will reply with a short step to confirm which installation is yours. Please keep the app installed until this is done.
  3. We delete your conversations, messages, preferences, notification token, plan record and usage counters within 30 days and confirm by email. Copies in backups disappear as those backups expire (see section 9).

You can also ask us to delete only part of your data, for example one conversation. Deleting your data does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel that in Google Play first (see our Terms of Service).

Request deletion by email

11Security

  • All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted with HTTPS/TLS.
  • The keys that sign your installation in are stored encrypted on your phone.
  • Lunova's data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your chats are not copied to other devices.
  • Access to our server and database is limited to the people who operate the service.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app, so please protect your phone with a screen lock.

12Where data is processed

Our server and database are located in Frankfurt, Germany. Our providers, including Google and Cloudflare, may process data in other countries, including the United States. Where required, such transfers rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses provided by those companies. By using Lunova you understand that your information may be processed outside the country where you live.

13Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of these rights:

  • Access: ask for a copy of the data we hold about your installation.
  • Correction: change your name and preferences in the app, or ask us to correct other data.
  • Deletion: see section 10.
  • Withdraw consent: switch off microphone or notification permission, or change your ad choices, at any time.
  • Object or restrict: ask us to stop or limit certain uses of your data.
  • Portability: ask for your data in a commonly used format.
  • Complain: contact your local data protection authority.

EEA, UK and Switzerland: these rights come from the GDPR and equivalent laws.

India: under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may access, correct and erase your personal data, raise a grievance with us, and nominate another person to exercise your rights. Send grievances to the contact in section 16. We aim to respond within 30 days.

United States, including California: you may request to know, delete and correct your personal information, and you will not be treated differently for doing so. We do not sell personal information. Ads on the free plan may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under California law. You can opt out through the ad settings described in section 7 or by moving to a paid plan.

To use any of these rights, email xypherlabs.dev@gmail.com. We may need to confirm which installation is yours before acting.
